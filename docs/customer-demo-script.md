# ClinWrite.AI · Customer Demo Script

**Duration:** 30 minutes · **Audience:** Medical/Regulatory writing leads, C-suite, IT/Procurement · **Format:** Screen-by-screen click path

**Live URL:** `https://proto.clinwrite.ai/`

---

## Before the meeting (5-min checklist)

1. Open `https://proto.clinwrite.ai/` in **Chrome incognito** (avoids stale service-worker cache from any prior session).
2. Verify persona is **Dr James Hartley (Admin — Global Clinical)**. If not, use the persona switcher in the top nav (avatar → persona list).
3. Confirm you land on the **VELORA-301** project dashboard automatically (single-project auto-forward).
4. Have this hero storyline in mind: *"A single oncology programme — VELORA-301 — moving from CSR to publication to promotional content to regulatory submission, on one platform, with one audit trail."*
5. Open a second incognito window at `https://proto.clinwrite.ai/kol-review/kol-tok-001-demo` — you'll switch to it during the Module E demo.

## Objection prompt sheet

| If they say...                                    | Respond with...                                                                                                    |
|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| "We already use Veeva / Vault"                    | "Vault stores documents. ClinWrite authors, reviews, and publishes them — end-to-end with role-scoped AI. It sits *in front of* Vault, not against it. We integrate." |
| "How is this different from Word + SharePoint?"   | "Try opening a locked paragraph in SharePoint. Try getting an audit trail out of it. Try enforcing an ICH framework across every document. That's what changes here." |
| "AI in regulated writing — is that even allowed?" | "Every AI action is logged with prompt, model, timestamp, and human accept/reject. FDA's own guidance permits AI-assisted authoring provided you can reconstruct human oversight. This platform *is* that reconstruction." |
| "What about our data?"                            | "Every client gets an isolated tenant. Your content never touches another client's model. We do not train on your data. Hosting can be UK, EU, or US — your call."       |
| "How fast could we be live?"                      | "Two weeks for a pilot on one programme. Ninety days for a production rollout including SSO, framework mapping, and user onboarding."                                    |
